# Owner approval checklist

- Preferred public name, owner story, business entity, address presentation, phone, and service area.
- Insurance wording, satisfaction process, chemicals/methods, payment methods, discounts, response expectations, and scheduling language.
- Whether vehicle cleaning remains a strategic service.
- Current service list and four-step process wording.
- Review excerpts and source links.
- Original logo, Terez portrait, truck/equipment images, matched before/after pairs, video masters, captions, and web-use permissions.
- Confirmation that no visible faces, house numbers, license plates, or private addresses lack approval.
- Production form processor, secure photo storage, retention, privacy terms, analytics, canonical domain, and redirects.
